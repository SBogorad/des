# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the project; and
- showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publication of private information without permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports may be sent privately through the repository owner's contact channel. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect confidentiality where practical, and may remove, edit, reject, or take other appropriate action on contributions and participation.
